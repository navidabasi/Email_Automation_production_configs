Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-11

This Privacy Policy explains how Navid Abasi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Email Automation desktop application (the "App").

Information We Access

  • Gmail data (read-only) to list messages and download attachments you authorize.
  • Google Sheets data to create or update spreadsheets you authorize.

How We Use Information

  • Extract PDF content and structured fields based on your configuration.
  • Export results to local Excel files.
  • Optionally write results to Google Sheets under your account.

Data Storage

  • All configuration and tokens are stored locally on your machine using the OS keyring.
  • Extracted files are stored locally in the directories you choose.
  • We do not transmit your data to any third-party servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

Data Retention

Data remains on your local machine until you delete it or remove the app.

Security

We use OS keyring storage for secrets and apply standard security measures (password hashing, admin TOTP, and rate limiting).

Your Choices

  • You may revoke Google access at any time from your Google Account.
  • You may delete local files and configs at any time.
